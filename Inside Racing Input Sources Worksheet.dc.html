Two questions to settle first

Do the four blocks in group A come off one source? Deadlines appear in all of pages 3–4, 5–25 and 26–46. If they are keyed or exported separately, that is where errors will be.

What horizon does each section cover? In the August edition the summary chart runs to late October but the detailed programs stop in early October. If that is a rule rather than a monthly call, it should be recorded here.

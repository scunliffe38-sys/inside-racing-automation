Page numbering and advertising · For noting

  • The contents page and page numbers are worked out by adding up each section's length. For ten sections (Entry Deadlines, Programs Summary Chart, Race Programs, Riders' Agents, Jump-Outs, Flat Trials, Division of Races, Victorian Race Series, Picnic Racing and From the Stewards' Room) the checker used last known lengths, because those pages report their real length only once they have been opened. If any of those sections has grown or shrunk this month, page numbers after it can be out by a page. The assembled edition corrects this itself when it lays out, so a quick check of the contents page against the PDF is enough.
  • No Ad Placements.csv was found, so only house advertising runs.
